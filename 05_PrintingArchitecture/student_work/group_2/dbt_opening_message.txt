============ Commands =============
==Low level Arduino Pin control==
mode <pin> <mode>: pinMode()
read <pin>: digitalRead()
aread <pin>: analogRead()
write <pin> <value>: digitalWrite()
awrite <pin> <value>: analogWrite()
out <outputchannel> <on/off>: 0-N_OUTCHANNELS_-1    /   0/1

==Temperature Control==
heater <number> <value>: 0/1 on/off   /   0-250C
